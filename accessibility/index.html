Accessibility

These pages exist only to finish connecting a Javaherian & Ruszecki employee's Google account to Einstein, the firm's assistant.

What we've done: plain HTML with real headings and links, text that scales with your device's font size, enough color contrast in light and dark mode, and nothing that depends on hovering or animation. The main button is a normal link, so screen readers announce it.

What we haven't done: the pages have not been tested with a screen reader or audited against WCAG by an outside reviewer.

If anything here is hard to use, text Einstein or tell Dylan, and we'll connect your account another way.
